### Gyan Kumar Sah

Final-year B.Sc. Physics (Honours with Research) student at SRM University-AP, from Mahottari, Nepal.

I work on experimental condensed-matter and device physics in the Device Physics Lab (with Dr. Krishna Prasad Maity) and as a Production Engineer at Shakti Photon Solutions. My work sits across three areas: lab hardware and instrument automation, quantum computing tooling, and local-first software.

#### Lab Instrumentation & Hardware Software

| Project | Summary | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| [Cryocon_22C_Temperature_Controller_Python-File](https://github.com/sahgyan9/Cryocon_22C_Temperature_Controller_Python-File) | RS-232 driver, Tkinter GUI, and PID overshoot study (reduced overshoot from +4.08 K to +0.22 K across 297–450 K) for a Janis ST-LN-500 cryogenic probe station. | Python, PySerial, Tkinter, SCPI, LabVIEW |
